          Delete synced data
          
Erases the synced copy of your feeds from the relays and stops syncing on this device. Feeds in this browser are kept. Other devices that are still signed in will upload their copy again the next time they sync.

          
        

      


      

        The heck is a Nostr?
        
This app uses the Nostr protocol for optional synchronization. If you'd like to read more about the protocol, you can do so here. Otherwise, you can just ignore that fact. Your feeds are stored as NIP-78 application data, End-To-End encrypted, so nobody can read them unless they have the sync key. If you ever lose it, you'll be unable to access your synced data; and if you give it to someone else, they'll be able to view and edit your feeds just like you. There's no server of mine involved, so there is no way to reset or change your sync key other than generating a new one and uploading your feeds to it again.
